# Acme Co. — Refund Policy

## How refunds work
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of delivery. Open the order in your account and choose "Request refund".
Once a refund is approved, it is processed within 5 business days and returned to the original payment method.

## What cannot be refunded
Gift cards, digital downloads that have been opened, and items marked "Final sale" cannot be refunded.
Shipping fees are refunded only if the item arrived damaged or was sent by mistake.

## Damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged, email support@acme.example with a photo within 7 days. We will send a replacement
or refund the full amount, including shipping, at no cost to you.
