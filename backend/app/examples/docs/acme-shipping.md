# Acme Co. — Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 12 dollars.
Orders placed before 2 pm on a weekday ship the same day.

## Free shipping
Orders over 50 dollars ship free with standard delivery inside the country.

## Tracking
A tracking link is emailed as soon as the order ships. If a package has not moved for 5 days, contact support
and we will open an investigation with the carrier.

## International orders
We ship to more than 40 countries. International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days. Customs duties and import
taxes are paid by the customer.
