# Acme Co. — Account and Security

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long. We recommend a password manager. Passwords do not expire, but
you should change yours if you think it was exposed.

## Two-factor authentication
You can turn on two-factor authentication under Settings, then Security. We support authenticator apps and
hardware keys. SMS codes are available but not recommended.

## Locked accounts
After 5 failed sign-in attempts your account is locked for 30 minutes. Use "Forgot password" to unlock it sooner.

## Deleting your account
Email privacy@acme.example from your registered address. We delete your personal data within 30 days.
